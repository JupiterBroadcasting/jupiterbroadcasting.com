# Jupiter Broadcasting

> Jupiter Broadcasting (JB) is a network of Linux and open source technology podcasts, including LINUX Unplugged, Linux Action News, Coder Radio, Self-Hosted, and Jupiter EXTRAS. The site hosts episode show notes plus pages about membership, live shows, sponsors, and community.

Each show has its own `llms.txt` under its path (e.g. `/show/linux-unplugged/llms.txt`) describing the show and linking its ten most recent episodes; the complete episode URL list for each show is JSON at `/show/<slug>/episodes.json`. Any page on the site is available as markdown by appending `index.md` to its URL (e.g. `/show/linux-unplugged/650/index.md`).
